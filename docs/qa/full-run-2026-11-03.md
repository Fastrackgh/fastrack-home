# Full UI run, 3 Nov 2026: Asante Hills Academy

A brand-new school was set up from nothing and taken through a term **through the real screens**, in a visible browser, as each role would use it. Every page and action was timed. Report page: https://claude.ai/artifact/Kv9QoTYt5mp2f3rAe5kxY3

## Result

- **156 checks: 150 pass, 4 fail, 2 pass with a problem.** On top of that, 66 timed page loads (every menu page for 5 roles) showed no errors and no browser script errors.
- **Release blocker, OFF-19 (High), since fixed:** PR #19 (80e85e2) merged the branches into main the same day; re-test 11 of 11 in [offline-sync-qa.md](./offline-sync-qa.md). Original finding: backend `main` (d55fa69) lacks PRs #16 and #17 (offline admissions and student edits). They merged into their stacked base branches, not main. With backend main and frontend main, admitting or editing a pupil fails with 400 `IDEMPOTENCY_NOT_SUPPORTED`. A plain merge of `e762195` into main also leaves `student-enrollment.service.ts` not compiling (17 errors; both sides changed it for QA-283), plus an add/add conflict in `test/sensitive-student-writes.e2e-spec.ts`. Details in [offline-sync-qa.md](./offline-sync-qa.md).
- **8 new findings, QA-284 to QA-291** (2 Medium, 6 Low), in [findings.md](./findings.md):
  - QA-285 (Medium, FE): closing the payment window at a warning leaves the refused payment in Needs review, reading "the GHS 50,000.00 is still in the cash drawer… Keep as credit".
  - QA-290 (Medium, product): 11 of 23 staff menu pages are UI previews with invented sample data, shown to a real school.
  - QA-284, 286, 287, 288, 289, 291 (Low): fee structure due date not marked required; extra bill pre-selects Tuition (QA-286 later withdrawn: the test script chose Tuition, not the app); staff Term totals count days before admission; offline register save takes 8.4 s; an over-maximum mark has no message; raw "ThrottlerException" on sign-in.
- **Known findings still open:** QA-229 (Paystack rejects `.test` parent emails, raw error) and QA-043 (a second discount silently replaces the first).
